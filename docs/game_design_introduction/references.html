参考文献

本書の執筆にあたって参考にした文献と、さらに学ぶための文献を挙げる。

ゲームデザイン全般

  • Jesse Schell, The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses, 3rd ed., CRC Press, 2019.

  • Tracy Fullerton, Game Design Workshop: A Playcentric Approach to Creating Innovative Games, 4th ed., CRC Press, 2018.

  • Katie Salen, Eric Zimmerman, Rules of Play: Game Design Fundamentals, MIT Press, 2003.

  • Raph Koster, A Theory of Fun for Game Design, 2nd ed., O'Reilly Media, 2013.

  • Ernest Adams, Joris Dormans, Game Mechanics: Advanced Game Design, New Riders, 2012.

  • Ian Schreiber, Brenda Romero, Game Balance, CRC Press, 2021.

  • Steve Swink, Game Feel: A Game Designer's Guide to Virtual Sensation, Morgan Kaufmann, 2008.

  • Martin Jonasson, Petri Purho, "Juice it or lose it", 講演, 2012.

  • Erik Fagerholt, Magnus Lorentzon, "Beyond the HUD: User Interfaces for Increased Player Immersion in FPS Games", Master's thesis, Chalmers University of Technology, 2009.

ゲームと遊びの理論

  • Robin Hunicke, Marc LeBlanc, Robert Zubek, "MDA: A Formal Approach to Game Design and Game Research", Proceedings of the AAAI Workshop on Challenges in Game AI, 2004. PDF

  • Jesper Juul, Half-Real: Video Games between Real Rules and Fictional Worlds, MIT Press, 2005.

  • Bernard Suits, The Grasshopper: Games, Life and Utopia, University of Toronto Press, 1978.

  • Roger Caillois, Les jeux et les hommes, Gallimard, 1958. （邦訳：ロジェ・カイヨワ『遊びと人間』、講談社学術文庫、1990 年）

  • Jane McGonigal, Reality Is Broken: Why Games Make Us Better and How They Can Change the World, Penguin Press, 2011.

プレイヤーと心理

  • Richard Bartle, "Hearts, Clubs, Diamonds, Spades: Players Who Suit MUDs", 1996. Web

  • Nicole Lazzaro, "Why We Play Games: Four Keys to More Emotion Without Story", XEODesign, 2004.

  • Edward L. Deci, "Effects of Externally Mediated Rewards on Intrinsic Motivation", Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 18(1), pp. 105-115, 1971.

  • 消費者庁「オンラインゲームの『コンプガチャ』と景品表示法の景品規制について」、2012 年。

  • Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi, Flow: The Psychology of Optimal Experience, Harper & Row, 1990.

  • Richard M. Ryan, Edward L. Deci, "Self-Determination Theory and the Facilitation of Intrinsic Motivation, Social Development, and Well-Being", American Psychologist, 55(1), pp. 68-78, 2000.

レベルデザイン

  • Christopher W. Totten, An Architectural Approach to Level Design, 2nd ed., CRC Press, 2019.

  • Scott Rogers, Level Up! The Guide to Great Video Game Design, 2nd ed., Wiley, 2014.

  • Christian Nutt, "The Structure of Fun: Learning from Super Mario 3D Land's Director", Gamasutra, 2012. （林田宏一へのインタビュー）

  • Noor Shaker, Julian Togelius, Mark J. Nelson, Procedural Content Generation in Games, Springer, 2016. Web

  • Tanya X. Short, Tarn Adams (eds.), Procedural Generation in Game Design, CRC Press, 2017.

  • Derek Yu, Spelunky, Boss Fight Books, 2016.

  • Amit Patel, Red Blob Games. Web （グラフ探索や地図の生成などを、動く図で解説している）
